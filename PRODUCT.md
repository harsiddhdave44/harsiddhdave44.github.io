# PRODUCT.md

## Product Purpose
Personal portfolio for Harsiddh Dave, Platform Engineer. Its single job: get
interviews and network conversations started. Every page argues one thesis:
this engineer thinks production-first (runtime behavior, failure modes,
debuggability), not config-first.

## Register
brand

## Users
- Technical recruiters skimming on a phone between calls: need role, seniority
  signal, and proof-of-work in under 30 seconds.
- Startup founders and engineering leaders reading deeper: judge how the
  candidate thinks, not just what tools they list.

## Brand & Tone
Calm operational confidence. The voice of a good incident report: precise,
concrete, zero hype. Vocabulary borrowed from production systems (status,
runbook, resolved, route). Dry humor allowed in system furniture (404 page),
never in the work itself.

## Design concept
"The Status Page": status-page and runbook vernacular rendered with
document-grade editorial typography. Signature element: case studies formatted
as production runbooks with mono metadata blocks.

## Anti-references
- Generic AI portfolio tells: warm-cream + serif + terracotta default, dark
  page with one neon accent, glowing cards, gradient text, hero-metric template.
- Terminal/CLI cosplay portfolios.
- SaaS landing page patterns (identical card grids, feature triads, testimonial
  carousels).

## Strategic principles
- Honesty is a feature: only claims from real work; in-progress work labeled.
- All personal content in src/content/ so the owner edits data, not components.
- Recruiter legibility beats cleverness whenever they conflict.
